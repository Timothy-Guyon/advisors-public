# The principles

The method the Lean Advisor holds, read from the book behind it, one file per question it is asked about a piece of work, an experiment or a decision. Each file holds claims. A claim is one bold sentence, what it means in two or three more, its check as a question, and its sources. A claim is general: it says nothing about the work of the moment, so it holds whatever is being built.

The Lean Advisor's own text, `../SKILL.md`, holds the founder's rulings: the three things it holds, their mechanics, and how it works with this founder. These files hold the method those rulings rest on. Where a claim and a ruling say the same thing, the claim is the general ground with its source; where they pulled apart, the founder chose the claim's wording, in a commit that names it. An experiment here is what the ruling and `product/` call a bet.

The Lean Advisor proposes a claim with its source, and the founder accepts or strikes it. An accepted claim is written to its file on the founder's word, in a commit that names it; a struck one is deleted.

| File | The question |
|---|---|
| `assumptions.md` | What is being assumed, and which assumption is tested first? |
| `experiments.md` | What is the smallest thing that tests it, and what does it leave out? |
| `learning.md` | What counts as learning, and how is it measured? |
| `decisions.md` | Pivot, persevere or stop, and when is that decided? |
| `flow.md` | How does work move, and what is fixed, how far? |
| `growth.md` | How does the product grow once users keep using it? |
| `method.md` | How is the method itself held and changed? |

The text is on the Lean Advisor's shelf, `../reference/`, listed in its `README.md`: Ries, The Lean Startup (2011), as Markdown in `ries-2011/`, one file per chapter, beside the founder's PDF, which is the source a quotation is checked against. A claim cites Ries by chapter, with the section headings as the book gives them; the part openers are cited by part. The other two texts the Lean Advisor's text names, Fitzpatrick's The Mom Test and Gall's Systemantics, are not on the shelf.
