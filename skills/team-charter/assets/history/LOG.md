# Bet log

One entry per bet, written at the retro when the bet closes. This is the only record of progress that counts: who used it, what happened, what we learned, and what we changed because of it. A retro with no user in it is a journal entry, not a retro.

The present lives in `work/BET.md`, the future in `work/LATER.md`, the validated product in `product/PRODUCT.md`, and the standing facts in `product/STANDING.md`. This file is history: an entry is added at a retro and never rewritten afterwards, and nothing that has to stay true lives here. An entry's skeleton and its words coined are written through the writer, `team.py entry` and `team.py coin`; the prose inside an entry is the advisor's.
