# Current bet

Read this before planning any work. One bet at a time. It is a draft until the client opens it, it closes on an event, not a date, and nothing else gets built until it closes.

- **Status:** no bet open.
- **Bet:** none yet.
- **Closes when:** none yet.

Keep the three status lines above to one short line each, under 300 characters. A ruling, a version, a tester's words or a change of any kind goes in its item below or in the log, never in a status line. When a bet opens, the status line starts "open, bet N", which the writer reads to find the open bet.

## The bet, item by item

1. **The bet.**
2. **The riskiest assumption.**
3. **The four questions.**
4. **The smallest experiment.**
5. **Who gets it, by name, with the date each agreed.**
6. **What we measure, what we ask, the prediction.**
7. **The event that closes it.**
8. **In scope and out of scope.**
9. **Definition of shipped.**
