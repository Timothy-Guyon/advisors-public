# Later

The parking lot. Anything the engineer wants to build that the current bet does not need goes here, with the trigger that would make it worth doing. Writing it down is how it stops occupying the head. Nothing here is forbidden. It is deferred until its trigger fires, or until the founder overrules and builds it anyway; either way the row exists first, and an overrule is dated in the row. Triggers follow proportional investment: a recurring pain fires its trigger on the second occurrence, felt by a user or costing the founder a session of real use, and what it unlocks is the smallest change that prevents a third, not the full solution.

Review at every retro. If a trigger has fired, promote the item into the next bet. If an item has sat through three bets with no trigger in sight, delete it.

Rows are added through the writer, `team.py park`, never by hand.
